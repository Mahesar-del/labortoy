Chemistry Testing FAQ

Answers to common questions about chemistry testing.

Why did my doctor order a BMP instead of a CMP?+

If your doctor only needs to check your kidney function, electrolytes, and blood sugar, a BMP is sufficient. A CMP is used when liver function also needs to be evaluated.

Can medications affect my BMP results?+

Yes, many medications (like diuretics or blood pressure pills) can affect electrolyte levels. Always inform your doctor about all medications you are taking.

At what age should I start getting a lipid panel?+

The American Heart Association recommends that all adults aged 20 or older have their cholesterol checked every 4 to 6 years. Those with cardiovascular disease risk factors may need more frequent testing.

How can I improve my lipid panel results?+

Lifestyle changes such as eating a heart-healthy diet, exercising regularly, losing weight, and quitting smoking can significantly improve your lipid profile.
